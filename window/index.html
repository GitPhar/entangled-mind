The Window The Entangled Mind · one belief state, from encoding to collapse

What you are looking at. Each glowing point is one claim from the language model's own short explanation of its answer to the question shown. Its brightness is the claim's amplitude, how strongly it is held in the quantum state. Its hue is its phase: cyan points toward yes, orange toward no, and grey points toward neither. Faint solid lines are the relation web the model's claims were judged to form (green supports, red contradicts). Dashed lines appear once the claims are entangled, and their opacity is the measured entanglement (negativity) of that pair. Drag the scrubber from encode through each evolve step to collapse, where the state is measured in the yes/no basis, and on to readout, where the archived runs of every arm are compared. The panel on the right shows the truth, the model's answer, and each arm's conflict score against its own pre-registered threshold. Compare the entangled arm A with the classical contradiction count E to see items where the entangling step loses a signal that plain counting keeps. Every stage here is an exact, noiseless state computed from the archived claims. The arm scores are the archived results, as run.
